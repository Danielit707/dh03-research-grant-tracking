# Stakeholder Analysis Matrix

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the stakeholder analysis matrix for the Research Opportunity, Proposal, and Project Management System. It categorizes internal and external key stakeholders, assessing their interest, influence, core expectations, primary concerns, and initial engagement strategy.

### Stakeholder Landscape Overview

- **Total Stakeholder Groups Identified:** 10
- **Key Decision Makers (High Interest / High Influence):** 5 (50%)
- **Primary Engagement Methods:** Meetings, scenario reviews, and specialized consultations.

--- 

## Stakeholder Analysis Matrix

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Principal Expectations | Main Concerns | Initial Engagement |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Principal Investigators** | `High` | `High` | Register and manage proposals, understand requirements, track status, respond to pending actions, and monitor funded projects | Unclear status, missed deadlines, repeated information, unclear approval requirements | Interview and lifecycle scenario review |
| **Research Team Members** | `High` | `Medium` | Access relevant proposal and project information, complete assigned activities, and understand responsibilities | Unclear responsibilities, limited visibility, outdated information | Scenario review and requirements consultation |
| **Research-office Coordinators** | `High` | `High` | Review submissions, validate documentation, coordinate institutional processes, monitor deadlines, and maintain proposal information | Incomplete submissions, manual follow-up, inconsistent records, unclear pending actions | Principal stakeholder meetings |
| **Research Director** | `High` | `High` | Monitor the research portfolio, oversee institutional processes, and obtain operational and strategic information | Lack of portfolio visibility, inconsistent processes, unreliable reporting | Principal stakeholder meetings and policy review |
| **Finance Representative** | `High` | `Medium` | Validate budgets, monitor permitted expenditures, and support financial reporting | Incorrect or outdated budget information, missing financial documentation, unclear approvals | Conditional consultation and workflow review |
| **Department Chair or Dean** | `Medium` | `High` | Review proposals requiring academic-unit approval and confirm relevant institutional commitments | Incomplete information, unclear responsibilities, late approval requests | Conditional consultation |
| **Research Ethics or Compliance Representative** | `Medium` | `High` | Review proposals requiring ethics, data-management, biosafety, or other institutional approvals | Missing evidence, incomplete compliance information, outdated approvals | Conditional consultation |
| **External Sponsor** | `Medium` | `High` | Define funding requirements, deadlines, deliverables, budget rules, and reporting expectations | Non-compliant proposals, missing information, missed deadlines, incorrect reporting | Scenario review and simulated integration consultation |
| **Information Technology Services** | `High` | `High` | Provide secure, maintainable, auditable, and supportable technical services and integrations | Weak authorization, inappropriate data handling, excessive complexity, fragile integrations | Architecture and security consultation |
| **Institutional / Senior Management** | `High` | `High` | Obtain institutional reports, monitor the research portfolio, understand overall status and risks, and support strategic decision-making | Incomplete or unreliable reporting, limited portfolio visibility, inconsistent information, and lack of traceability | Milestone reviews and reporting consultation |
