# Product Vision and Scope: DH-03 Research Project and Grant Tracking System

---

## 1. Vision Statement
**Horizon University** will provide a centralized and secure system for managing the lifecycle of research proposals and funded projects through a consistent and traceable process[cite: 1].

The solution will help researchers and authorized university personnel understand the status and pending actions of proposals and projects, coordinate required reviews and approvals, monitor important dates and deliverables, manage approved changes, and preserve a reliable history from proposal registration through project closure[cite: 1].

---

## 2. Current Problem
The current management of research proposals and funded projects presents several recurring difficulties[cite: 1]:

* **Fragmented & Inconsistent Information:** Proposals, documentation, deadlines, and budget versions circulate across different unstructured channels (emails, documents, and spreadsheets)[cite: 1].
* **Lack of Review Visibility:** Institutional reviews occur via unstructured email conversations, preventing researchers from identifying pending reviews, approvals, or next actions[cite: 1].
* **Difficult Progress Monitoring:** Project milestones, reporting deadlines, and deliverables are hard to track, complicating the identification of overdue work or projects at risk[cite: 1].
* **Untraceable Changes & Decisions:** Modifications to project scope, schedule, team, or budget are not consistently documented, making decision and approval history hard to reconstruct[cite: 1].
* **Confidentiality & Access Risks:** Proposal information is shared with unauthorized personnel due to inconsistent access controls[cite: 1].
* **High Manual Overhead:** Management reporting is prepared manually, and closed projects often lack complete technical and administrative evidence[cite: 1].

---

## 3. Product Objectives
The initial software solution should[cite: 1]:

1. **Centralize Information:** Manage research opportunities, proposals, relevant documents, versions, investigators, and collaborators in one place[cite: 1].
2. **Provide Visibility:** Offer real-time tracking of proposal and project statuses, stages, pending actions, reviews, approvals, and key dates[cite: 1].
3. **Coordinate Reviews:** Streamline institutional review and approval processes throughout the proposal lifecycle[cite: 1].
4. **Monitor Deadlines:** Track submission dates, project milestones, and deliverables to support timely execution[cite: 1].
5. **Ensure Traceability:** Record and preserve critical changes, revisions, and activities across the entire lifecycle[cite: 1].
6. **Enforce Confidentiality:** Maintain role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure information visibility aligns with user responsibilities[cite: 1].
7. **Support Decision-Making:** Generate operational and institutional reports for management and leadership[cite: 1].

## 5. In Scope
The system will encompass the following capabilities[cite: 1]:

* Research opportunity registration and tracking[cite: 1].
* Research proposal creation and submission[cite: 1].
* Proposal information and required-document management[cite: 1].
* Document version management and traceability[cite: 1].
* Identification of principal investigators, research team members, and collaborators[cite: 1].
* Submission deadline and important-date tracking[cite: 1].
* Completeness review of proposal information and documents[cite: 1].
* Institutional review and approval coordination[cite: 1].
* Configurable approval requirements based on proposal or project characteristics[cite: 1].
* Proposal status and pending-action tracking[cite: 1].
* Recording of sponsor decisions and proposal outcomes[cite: 1].
* Activation of funded research projects[cite: 1].
* Project milestone and deliverable tracking[cite: 1].
* Project status and important-date monitoring[cite: 1].
* Controlled recording of approved project changes[cite: 1].
* Role-based visibility and confidentiality controls[cite: 1].
* Notifications for relevant status changes, deadlines, and pending actions[cite: 1].
* Search and filtering of proposals and projects[cite: 1].
* Operational and institutional reporting[cite: 1].
* Traceable history from proposal registration through project closure[cite: 1].
* Simulated integration with relevant institutional or external services[cite: 1].

---

## 6. Out of Scope
The system will **NOT** include or attempt to[cite: 1]:

* Replace the complete university financial system[cite: 1].
* Process real financial payments[cite: 1].
* Calculate institutional payroll[cite: 1].
* Manage purchasing or procurement processes[cite: 1].
* Replace the complete institutional document-management platform[cite: 1].
* Submit proposals directly to real external sponsor portals[cite: 1].
* Perform automated plagiarism detection[cite: 1].
* Conduct scientific peer review of research quality[cite: 1].
* Replace the dedicated research ethics review platform[cite: 1].
* Store real confidential research data[cite: 1].
* Manage laboratory experiments or hardware[cite: 1].
* Implement a full Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system for sponsors[cite: 1].
* Predict which proposals will receive funding[cite: 1].
* Support every sponsor policy used worldwide[cite: 1].
* Replace institutional systems for identity, finance, document management, or research compliance[cite: 1].

---

## 7. Initial Success
The project will be considered successful when authorized users can demonstrate a complete research lifecycle from proposal registration through institutional review, approval, sponsor decision, and, when funded, project activation and monitoring—including status visibility, document/version management, important dates, approved changes, and a fully traceable audit history[cite: 1].
